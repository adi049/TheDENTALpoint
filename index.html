Our Story

Care that remembers your name — not just your chart.

The Dental Point was founded by Dr. Pooja Gaur on a simple idea: that a visit to the dentist shouldn't feel clinical and cold. Over fifteen years, that idea has grown into a practice trusted by generations of families in Chirag Dilli and across South Delhi.

Every treatment plan starts with listening — to your concerns, your budget, and your time. We combine modern equipment with an unhurried, honest approach, so you always know exactly what's being done and why.
